what_i_learned: |
  Today we attended the AWS Summit and it was a different kind of day from our usual research sessions.
  Walking through the summit, it was genuinely impressive to see the range of companies present and the scale of what was on   display. That said, it was a lot to take in. The sheer number of people and the energy of the space made it feel slightly    overwhelming at some point.
  One thing that was hard to miss — AI was everywhere. Every booth, every panel, every pitch. AI this, AI that. At a certain   point it started to blur together, and it became clear that for a lot of companies, AI is more of a buzzword right now       than a specific solution. That was an interesting observation to sit with, especially coming from a program where we're      doing the actual hands-on work behind those kinds of claims.
  Overall though, it was a worthwhile experience. Seeing the industry landscape up close — the companies, the products, the    conversations — added context to the research we've been doing all summer and served as a good reminder of where this work   eventually lands in the real world.
