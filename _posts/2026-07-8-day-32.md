what_i_learned: |   
  Started the day by running our FastAPI application through a broader set of test images. The results were decent — not       perfect, but encouraging enough to confirm that the pipeline is holding up across different inputs. Testing on multiple      images rather than just one or two gives a more honest picture of how the app will perform in practice, so it was a          worthwhile exercise before we move into final polishing.

  After that we shifted to presentation work. Spent time updating our individual progress slides to reflect where the          project currently stands and making sure everything from the data pipeline through the FastAPI deployment is represented     accurately. With the final presentation coming up Friday, getting the slides right is just as important as the technical     work itself.

  After lunch we took a break from the research grind to watch Mercy, a film centered around an AI judge. It was genuinely     thrilling. It was the kind of movie that keeps you thinking well after it ends. The biggest takeaway for me was something    that actually connects back to our own project: both humans and AI are imperfect. Neither has the full picture on its own.   The most interesting space is where the two complement each other, human judgment filling in what AI misses, and AI          processing at a scale no human could manage alone. That tension is something we're navigating directly in our own work,      where we're benchmarking our model against human ATC-20 inspection standards rather than replacing them.
