  Got the day off after the two day symposium push, which was well deserved rest. But if I am being honest, today ended up     being one of the more emotionally heavy days of the entire program.

  Watching everyone leave one after the other hit differently than I expected. Six weeks is not a long time on paper, but      when you spend that time working through hard problems together, debugging code at odd hours, practicing presentations,      sitting through lectures, eating lunch together every day and sharing the small moments in between, something real builds    up. These are people I had never met before the start of June, and saying goodbye to them today felt genuinely painful. It   is one of those things that is hard to explain until you have experienced it yourself.

  We made the most of the last day together by heading out to Round 1 at Towson Town Center for one final outing. We went      bowling, laughed a lot, and soaked up the last few hours before everyone had to go their separate ways. It was the perfect   way to close out the summer. Simple, fun, and full of the same energy that made this cohort special from the beginning.

  At the end of the day though, everyone has their own path to get back to. Their own schools, their own research, their own   next chapters. That is how it is supposed to be. I am deeply grateful for this opportunity. To the coordinators who kept     everything running, to Dr. Steve and Dr. John for pushing our team to do work we are genuinely proud of, to every            participant who brought their full self to this program every single day. You all made this summer something I will carry    for a long time.
