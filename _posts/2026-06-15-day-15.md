what_i_learned: |
  First day of the in-person cohort and it was great to finally meet everyone face to face after weeks on Zoom.
  The morning covered data analysis and analytics fundamentals, a brief intro to the ML pipeline, and hands-on work with NumPy arrays, Pandas DataFrames, and basic Matplotlib visualizations. Data preprocessing was touched on as well, including handling null values and cleaning data.
  The afternoon shifted to AI bias, which was one of the more thought-provoking sessions so far. We discussed how AI systems can perform better for some demographics than others, effectively marginalizing underrepresented groups. The point about Africa having the largest representation gap in training data was a standout moment, and the activity that followed drove the point home in a concrete way.
  We wrapped up with the marshmallow activity, which was easily the most fun thing we did all day, followed by a walk over to the research labs where our professors gave us a tour of campus.
