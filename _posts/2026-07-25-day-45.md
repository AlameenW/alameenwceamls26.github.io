what_i_learned: |   
  Today had a good mix of wrapping up loose ends and actually having some fun.

  Started the morning in the research lab picking up where we left off on the paper. The focus was on getting all of our       results properly documented and formatted into the paper. Having the evaluation metrics, confusion matrices, and model       comparisons all sitting in the results section in a clean, readable format is a big step toward a complete paper. We made    solid progress and by 11am had reached a good stopping point.

  From there we moved into an info session about the upcoming symposium. We got a clearer picture of the format,               expectations, and logistics for the final two-day event. With the symposium being the culmination of everything we have      built and researched over the summer, it was useful to have all the details laid out clearly so we know exactly what to      prepare for and what to expect on the day.

  After that it was time for the SAIRI field day, which was a genuinely fun way to spend the afternoon. We played flag         football, did a marshmallow on a spoon race, played soccer, and a bunch of other activities. At this point in the program    with the symposium right around the corner, having an afternoon to decompress, laugh, and compete with the rest of the       cohort was exactly what everyone needed. The energy was great and it was a good reminder that the people you do research     alongside matter just as much as the research itself.
